A founder-described international invitation signals ambition, but the exact event organizer, status and itinerary still require confirmation.

An international opportunity, according to the founder

Luis Altamirano says he was invited to participate in a China-based event he identifies as “EPEC.” He connects the invitation to broader entrepreneurial and technology conversations following his public-facing founder activities. This publication has not reviewed a formal invitation, identified an official EPEC organizer or independently verified the proposed travel or participation.

That distinction matters. A founder reporting an invitation is different from an organizer confirming a place on an official program. No claim of confirmed attendance, speaking placement or institutional endorsement is made here.

Why international exposure interests technology startups

China is home to major manufacturing, electronics and robotics supply chains. For a founder exploring physical AI products, meetings with suppliers, operators and technical partners could be relevant to the early research phase, depending on the event and the nature of the introduction.

International conversations alone, however, do not establish a partnership or a commercial agreement. Meaningful progress would require documentation of the relevant organizations and follow-on work.

Clarifying EPEC and APEC

Readers should not assume that an event identified as “EPEC” is part of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) calendar. The names are similar but no connection has been established in the information supplied for this story.

Before a finalized international-event announcement is made, the organizers’ full name, official website, dates, invitation terms and consent to publish should be confirmed.

A next step, not a completed milestone

Altamirano has described plans that connect software, AI and robotics under SEAINT. If an international visit proceeds, documented discussions and concrete product development outcomes will provide the evidence needed for an expanded account.

Until then, this update records the founder’s stated invitation, with the relevant verification still outstanding.
