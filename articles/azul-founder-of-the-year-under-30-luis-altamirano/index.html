AZUL organizers describe a Founder of the Year Under 30 recognition for Luis Altamirano, with context on SEAINT and its next steps.

A recognition tied to the AZUL event

According to information provided for this publication by the organizers, AZUL Tech Day is recognizing technology entrepreneur Luis Altamirano under the title “Founder of the Year — Under 30.” The honor is described as an AZUL event recognition, rather than a national ranking or an award conferred by an unrelated news organization.

The organizer-supplied account connects the recognition to Altamirano’s stated interest in building businesses across enterprise software, artificial intelligence and emerging technologies. It does not include an independently reviewed judging scorecard, a recipient acceptance record or external confirmation of a finalized presentation.

The founder’s business interests

Altamirano identifies himself as a co-founder of SEAINT, a technology venture developing software and automation concepts for business workflows. He has also described activity across investing and sports-related businesses. Specific ownership, revenue and transaction claims should be evaluated from company and transaction records, rather than inferred from publicity materials.

The strategy he presents is to connect useful software to the routine but fragmented processes businesses still manage by phone, email and spreadsheets. Products built around those workflows must ultimately be judged on real adoption, reliability and paying customers.

Not the Forbes 30 Under 30 list

This recognition is presented as an AZUL Tech Day award. It is not a Forbes 30 Under 30 selection and should not be represented as one. The title “Under 30” describes the organizer’s award category, not a third-party credential.

A complete event record would normally include the award criteria, judging process, recipient list and presentation details. Publishing those documents would give readers a clearer way to evaluate the recognition.
