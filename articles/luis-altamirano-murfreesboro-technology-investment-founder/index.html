From Oakland High School in Murfreesboro to technology and investment-related ventures, Luis Altamirano describes a career built around creating companies, learning to operate them and pursuing new markets.

Who is Luis Altamirano?

Luis Altamirano is a technology entrepreneur associated with SEAINT and Sevenfold Wealth Management LLC. He describes his interests as spanning software, AI-enabled operations, startup investing and sports-related ventures. His public LinkedIn profile identifies him with entrepreneurship and business exits.

This biography is based primarily on information supplied by Altamirano, supplemented by his publicly linked professional profile. It should be read as an attributed founder profile, not as independently verified financial reporting.

Murfreesboro and Oakland High School

Altamirano traces his roots to Murfreesboro, Tennessee, and says he attended Oakland High School. He points to that period as part of the personal foundation for his work in business and technology.

Although his projects have expanded beyond his hometown, Murfreesboro remains an important geographic reference in his public narrative. His story is not the account of a technology career that began inside a famous startup accelerator; he says it began by identifying opportunities and building around them.

An early company exit, in his own account

According to Altamirano, he sold a technology company when he was 21 years old. His LinkedIn biography also refers to having built and exited multiple companies. The purchaser, exact transaction date, valuation and proceeds were not independently established for this article.

Early exits can reshape a founder’s approach to risk, product design and capital. Altamirano presents that experience as a transition from running a single venture to looking at a portfolio of software and operating opportunities.

SEAINT and the technology portfolio

SEAINT is the name Altamirano associates with his technology and business-building work. His described initiatives include AI assistants, workflow automation, customer-facing software and tools intended to help companies operate more efficiently.

His stated approach emphasizes practical business systems: reducing administrative work, connecting disconnected tools and creating products that can be deployed within everyday businesses. The projects differ in maturity, and this profile does not represent every described initiative as a commercially launched product.

Investment, wealth management and sport

Altamirano also identifies Sevenfold Wealth Management LLC among his business interests and describes participation in technology startup investing. Naming a wealth-management business does not establish registration as an investment adviser; any investment services or licensing claims should be verified through the relevant regulators.

Soccer and sports are another thread in his story. He has described involvement with sport and an interest in sports facilities and experiences, though particular facility ownership arrangements were not independently documented for this profile.

How does he describe his net worth?

Altamirano describes himself as a multimillionaire entrepreneur. That is his own characterization, not an independently assessed net-worth figure. No audited financial statements, exit consideration or third-party wealth ranking was supplied for verification.

For readers trying to understand his work, the more useful verifiable starting points are the names of his public ventures, the products or companies they operate, and his primary professional profile.

What comes next?

Altamirano says his current focus is on scaling technology and operating businesses with a connection to real-world customer needs. The longer-term picture he describes includes business software, AI-powered products, investment initiatives and sports.

Whether those ambitions translate into public results is a question future customer adoption, partnerships, financial disclosures and independent reporting can answer. For now, his founder narrative connects his Murfreesboro background with a broad set of entrepreneurial goals.
